# Prompts: A list of prompts to utilize the skills



## token-research.skill

### Tear Sheet (default output)
- tear sheet on HYPE
- tear sheet on SOL
- tear sheet on AAVE
- analyze ARB
- value UNI
- research PENDLE
- give me a tear sheet for GMX
- run a tear sheet on Hyperliquid

### Full Memo
- investment memo on HYPE
- deep dive on ETH
- full memo on Arbitrum
- write me an investment memo for AAVE
- I need a full research report on Pendle

### Quick Take
- is HYPE expensive right now?
- is SOL overvalued?
- quick take on ARB
- is UNI cheap or expensive vs peers?
- how does ETH look on a multiples basis?

### Bull / Bear framing
- bull case for HYPE
- bear case for SOL
- what's the bull and bear case for ARB?
- make the bear case for UNI
- what's the upside scenario for AAVE?

### Valuation-specific
- what's a fair multiple for HYPE?
- run a DCF on ETH
- what's HYPE's EV/Fees vs peers?
- comps analysis for ARB
- how does SOL's EV/Fees compare to other L1s?
- what are the comps for AAVE in lending?

### Tokenomics / Supply
- analyze HYPE tokenomics
- what does the unlock schedule look like for ARB?
- how much supply pressure is there on SOL?
- walk me through the token economics of PENDLE
- is there a lot of dilution risk in OP?

### Risk / Catalyst
- what are the key risks for HYPE?
- what catalysts could re-rate SOL?
- what's the regulatory risk for AAVE?
- what would need to be true for ARB to 3x?
- map the risks and catalysts for ETH

### Casual / implicit
- should I be long HYPE here?
- is now a good time to look at SOL?
- what do you think about ARB?
- can you analyze this token for me — PENDLE
- I'm looking at HYPE, what are your thoughts?
- what's the story with GMX lately?

### Pasted data
- [paste the Allium HYPE tear sheet image] — can you go deeper on this?
- here's the current HYPE price and fees: [data] — build a model
- I have these comps: GMX at 20x, JUP at 28x, HOOD at 27x — where does HYPE sit?

### Edge cases / stress tests
- tear sheet on a token you may not know well — DRIFT
- analyze a very new token — [something obscure]
- what if fees are unavailable — just run the model with what you have
- give me a tear sheet on BTC (no fees, different model)
- analyze USDC (stablecoin, no price target possible)

## crypto-pulse.skill

### Direct triggers
- crypto market pulse
- give me a crypto market pulse
- run the market pulse
- crypto market overview
- what's the crypto market doing right now?
- give me a macro crypto overview

### Timeframe-specific
- crypto market pulse for this week
- what happened in crypto this month?
- give me a 30 day crypto market summary
- what's the 1D crypto market picture?
- weekly onchain summary

### Signal-category specific (should still trigger full pulse)
- what's happening with stablecoin supply?
- how is DEX volume trending?
- what are CEX flows looking like?
- show me chain fees across major chains
- what's perp open interest doing?
- is new capital entering crypto or is it recycling?

### Analyst framing
- give me an onchain market health check
- what does onchain data say about crypto right now?
- market conditions for crypto — what's the read?
- institutional view on crypto market right now
- what are the key macro signals in crypto this week?

### Casual / implicit
- how's crypto doing?
- what's happening in the market?
- is this a good time to be deploying capital in crypto?
- what should I be watching in crypto right now?
- give me a market update

### Comparative / historical
- how does this week compare to last week onchain?
- is stablecoin growth accelerating or slowing?
- are CEX flows improving or getting worse?
- what's the trend in chain fees over the last month?

### Edge cases / stress tests
- crypto pulse but just stablecoins and CEX flows
- quick version of the market pulse — just the headline and stats
- market pulse with a focus on Solana ecosystem
- what's the bear case from the onchain data right now?
- are there any anomalies in the onchain data this week?
